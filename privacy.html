Privacy Policy 🐰✨

Welcome to Bunny's Journey. Your privacy matters to us, and we want this magical experience to feel safe, cozy, and transparent for everyone 💖

1. Information We Collect

When you use this website, we may collect:

  • Your supporter name (if provided).
  • Donation/support amount.
  • Basic analytics data such as device type, browser, pages visited, and interactions.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Improve the bunny journey experience and animation flows.
  • Display supporter rankings and live progress on the leaderboard.
  • Understand website performance and visitor interactions.
  • Keep the website secure, functional, and spam-free.

3. Cookies & Local Storage 🍪

We use **Local Storage** and **Cookies** to enhance your experience. This includes saving your cookie preference choice (so the banner doesn't pop up every time you refresh) and temporarily holding your pending donation details (amount and name) while you switch to the Stripe secure payment page. No personal identifier data is ever sold or shared maliciously.

4. Secure Payments

Payments are securely processed through third-party payment providers like **Stripe**. We do not process or store your financial card information on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

To keep the website functional, we use trusted services including:

  • Firebase: For live data syncing of the bunny's journey progress.
  • Google Analytics: To understand anonymous visitor trends.
  • Stripe: For secure payment infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare: For website speed and security.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, you can contact us at: support@getbunny.me
